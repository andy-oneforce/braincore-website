
ASIOS is a **Platform that installs a per-user Workspace**. You run one Workspace; the Platform is
what installs and updates it. Everything you do day to day — capturing an idea, working a
conversation, building something new — happens inside that Workspace, on top of a small set of
ideas that stay consistent no matter what you're working on.

This guide gets you from "I just installed this" to "I understand how the pieces fit together,"
in two short sections:

- **[Getting started](./getting-started/index.md)** walks through installing your Workspace and
  taking your first few actions in it.
- **[Concepts](./concepts/layers.md)** explains the two ideas worth understanding early — **layers**
  (who owns a piece of content and where it ships) and **activities** (the unit everything you do
  is tracked as).

You don't need to read this guide end to end before using ASIOS. Skim Getting started, keep
Concepts as a reference, and come back to either section whenever something doesn't click.

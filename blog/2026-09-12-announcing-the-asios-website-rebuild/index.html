Announcing the ASIOS website rebuild

Andy Zhulenev · 2026-09-12

  • Announcement

Today we're shipping the rebuilt ASIOS website: a marketing home, a documentation section, and this blog, all served from the same static build.

What's new

The site now runs on three page templates — marketing, docs, and blog — sharing one design system and one accent-only colour palette. Docs carry a collapsible sidebar and a per-page table of contents; the blog carries tags, pagination, and related-post suggestions.

Why a rebuild, not a redesign

The previous site had accumulated enough one-off pages that no single template covered them. Rebuilding from three templates, rather than patching the old structure, means every new page — doc, post, or marketing page — starts from a known-good shape instead of a fresh set of decisions.

Expect the docs section to grow first, followed by a steadier blog cadence covering how the site itself gets built.
